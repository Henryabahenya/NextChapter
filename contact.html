FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the most common questions about NextChapter Innovation, our services, and how we work.

What services does NextChapter Innovation offer?

We specialize in web development, graphic design, branding, UI/UX design, digital skills training, AI solutions, and technology consulting.

Do you work with organizations outside Kenya?

Yes. We collaborate with clients, organizations, and partners locally and internationally through remote and hybrid engagements.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on the project scope. Smaller projects may take a few days, while larger digital solutions can take several weeks.

Do you provide digital skills training?

Yes. We offer training in web development, graphic design, AI, digital literacy, and other technology-related skills.

How can we become a partner?

Simply contact us through the contact form, email, or WhatsApp. We'd be happy to explore opportunities for collaboration.
